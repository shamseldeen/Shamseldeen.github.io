Why a growing pharmacy chain loses money

Problem: a 1,000-branch chain sold more every year and still lost money. What I did: followed every sale from the till to one clear report. Result: the chain was paying more for 248 products than it sold them for.

  • 114M SAR lost on those 248 products (2020–2025); every other product earns about 33%
  • A fix for each product: the supplier discount needed to make it profitable again
  • No one blamed without proof: 7 fraud patterns checked, none found

Why a drug distributor missed its target

Problem: a Saudi drug distributor reached 97.2% of target, SAR 7.0M short. What I did: a 6-page Power BI report on sales, targets, discounts and stock. Result: the sales team was not the cause; discounts (2x the gap) and stock in the wrong place were.

  • 68 of 72 agents within 5% of target: a shared cause, not weak people
  • SAR 14.1M in discounts against a SAR 7.0M gap
  • 27 stockouts while SAR 825K of stock was near expiry
