Findings

I spent years reading prescriptions, checking dosages against symptoms, and catching the error before it reached the patient. That instinct — verify first, then act — turned out to be exactly what data work demands.

Today I apply the same discipline to sales, customers and store performance: clean the dataset the way you'd double-check a dose, look for what doesn't fit, and turn the pattern into something a manager can act on the same afternoon. My background spans pharmacy and retail operations, which means I don't just write the query — I understand why the business is asking it.
